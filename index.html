Sources & Method

Numeric full-PPR projections (attempts, receptions, FPTS) come from the FantasyPros consensus draft-season projection model, pulled Sept 3–5, 2026 — used consistently so the numbers that exist are comparable to each other. FantasyPros only fully renders its top tier of backs and receivers to automated tools, so most players carry a qualitative tier (from FTN Fantasy's 2026 RB tiers) and a role description instead of a fabricated point total. Depth charts and role calls are cross-checked against team-released unofficial depth charts, beat-reporter reporting, and multiple fantasy outlets; committee/injury situations note disagreement explicitly. Workload splits marked "est." are this document's own estimate from the reporting below, not a stat a team publishes — treat them as directional, not exact.

    Built for a 2026 full-PPR redraft. Depth charts move fast in early September — re-check anything flagged 🔴 or ⭐⭐⭐ the morning of your draft. Not betting or injury-diagnosis advice.
